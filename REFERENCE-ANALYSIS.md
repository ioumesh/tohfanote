## Boundaries and design decisions

The reference homepage advertises ₹199, UPI payment, 90-day retention, free previews and midnight opening. Those are observed marketing statements; payment, retention and server enforcement were not independently verified. Other categories were inventoried from public links, not investigated end to end. Some intermediate REA captures hit changing/duplicate Continue controls; final successful captures above use the visible sticky action and retain that boundary.

You Are Special uses original copy, CSS/SVG artwork, chime compositions and branding. It is free. It uses a separate protected management link and server-enforced scheduling. Its seven occasions use a shared builder; Birthday has candles, Anniversary has creator-editable milestones, Proposal has a considerate local response reveal, Photo Puzzle has a working 3×3 swap puzzle, and Love, Best Friend and Apology center on the personalized gallery and letter. These are implementation choices, not claims about the reference's internals.
